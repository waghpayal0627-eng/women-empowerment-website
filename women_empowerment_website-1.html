ABOUT WOMEN EMPOWERMENT

Creating Awareness Through Technology

Women Empowerment is a Community Engagement Project designed to connect education, digital literacy, career awareness and skill development.

Problem Statement

Many students and community members may have limited awareness about educational opportunities, scholarships, government schemes, digital tools and career paths.

Our Objectives

  • Improve education awareness
  • Promote digital literacy
  • Increase scholarship awareness
  • Provide career guidance
  • Encourage skill development

Why We Chose This Topic

Education and awareness can help individuals understand available opportunities and make informed decisions about learning, careers and personal development.
